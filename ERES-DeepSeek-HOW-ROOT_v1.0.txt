TITLE
Operational Validation of HOW ≡ ROOT-HPE (ERES Def-Rel v1.0)

DESCRIPTION
This study tests whether HOW, defined as the root method of
Human-Performance-Enhancement, is empirically observable under
strict operational definitions. HOW is declared operating (H = 1)
when a self produces endogenous elevation above its own mediocrity
baseline across a fixed 30-minute window with zero external input,
certified by a blind Center-of-Excellence via a stress-to-color
readout. This is a falsifiable specification; it does not assume
the framework is true.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. RESEARCH QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
RQ1: Can H = 1 be observed reliably under the defined protocol?
RQ2: Does endogenous elevation (E) hold ≥ M + 1 SD across W?
RQ3: Does a blind Center-of-Excellence certify consistently?
RQ4: Does the stress-to-color readout (S2C) track PSS?
RQ5: Does the HOW condition outperform an active control?

------------------------------------------------------------
2. HYPOTHESES
------------------------------------------------------------
H1: H = 1 in ≥ 70% of HOW-condition test sessions.
H2: In H = 1 sessions, E(t) ≥ M + 1 SD for ≥ 90% of W.
H3: CoE certification ICC(2,k) ≥ .80.
H4: S2C correlates with PSS at r ≥ .70.
H5: HOW > active control on E, d ≥ 0.50.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. STUDY DESIGN
------------------------------------------------------------
Design: 3-arm randomized controlled trial.
Arms: (1) HOW, (2) Active control, (3) Waitlist.
Phases: 14-day baseline; 7-day Purist Form training;
5 × 30-min test sessions; 14-day follow-up.
Randomization: Block, 1:1:1, stratified by baseline M.
Blinding: Participants not blind; raters and analysts blind.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. SAMPLING PLAN
------------------------------------------------------------
N = 90 (30/arm). Power = .80, alpha = .05, d = 0.50.
Inclusion: 18–65; no psychiatric diagnosis; no substance use.
Exclusion: Meditation practice > 1 hr/day.
Recruitment: [source]. Stopping: interim at N = 45;
stop if adverse events > 10% or CoE ICC < .60.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. VARIABLES
------------------------------------------------------------
Primary: H (binary); E(t) (continuous).
Secondary: G (goodness); S2C (stress-to-color);
PF fidelity; CoE certification (1/0).
Exploratory: HRV-RMSSD; EDA; follow-up durability.
Instruments:
- PANAS (Watson et al., 1988)
- WHO-5 (WHO, 1998)
- FSS-2 (Jackson & Eklund, 2002)
- PSS-10 (Cohen et al., 1983)
- VIA-IS (Peterson & Seligman, 2004)
- Moral Elevation Scale (Haidt, 2003)
- EMA 4x/day (Shiffman et al., 2008)
- HRV-RMSSD (Polar H10 / Empatica E4)
- EDA (Empatica E4)

Operational definitions:
M = 14-day median composite.
E(t) = mean of z(PA), z(WHO5), z(FSS2), z(G), -z(PSS).
G = .50*z(prosocial) + .50*z(VIA strengths use).
S = z(PSS) + z(HRV-RMSSD reversed) + z(EDA).
S2C = CIELAB map of normalized S.
PF fidelity = criteria correctly applied / 5.
H = 1 iff E(t) ≥ M + 1SD for all t in W; X = 0;
CoE = 1; G ≥ G-bar + 1SD; PF fidelity ≥ .80.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. ANALYSIS PLAN
------------------------------------------------------------
Primary: Mixed-effects logistic regression,
H ~ condition + (1|subject).
Secondary: Linear mixed models for E(t), G, S2C.
Reliability: ICC(2,k) for CoE.
Correlation: Pearson r for S2C vs PSS.
Effect size: Cohen's d, HOW vs active control.
Missing data: Multiple imputation, m = 20.
Software: R 4.x (lme4, irr, psych) or Python 3.x.
Inference: alpha = .05, two-tailed; corrections for
multiple comparisons via Benjamini-Hochberg.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. FALSIFICATION CRITERIA
------------------------------------------------------------
HOW is falsified if:
- H = 1 in < 30% of HOW sessions;
- CoE ICC < .60;
- S2C vs PSS r < .40;
- No difference vs active control, d < 0.20.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. OTHER
------------------------------------------------------------
Limitations: X = 0 is enforced, not metaphysically proven;
PF is self-authored; CoE independence is structural;
S2C is a proxy; H is binary by decision.
Deviations log: maintained during study.
