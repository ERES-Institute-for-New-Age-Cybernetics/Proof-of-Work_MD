TITLE
Operational Validation of HOW ≡ ROOT-HPE (ERES Def-Rel v1.0):
A Three-Arm Randomized Trial of Endogenous Elevation

DESCRIPTION
This study tests whether HOW, defined as the root method of
Human-Performance-Enhancement, is empirically observable under
strict operational definitions. HOW is declared operating (H = 1)
in a test session when a participant's momentary elevation
composite E(t) stays at or above their own baseline ("mediocrity")
threshold at every in-window probe of a fixed 30-minute window,
under a documented zero-external-input protocol (X = 0), with
Purist Form fidelity ≥ .80 and certification by a blind
Center-of-Excellence (CoE) panel reading a physiology-only
stress-to-color (S2C) trace. This is a falsifiable specification;
it does not assume the framework is true. Outcomes are classified
in advance as Supported, Falsified, Inconclusive (instrument
failure), or Indeterminate.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. RESEARCH QUESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------
RQ1: Does HOW training raise the rate of H = 1 sessions relative
     to an attention-matched active control?
RQ2: In the HOW arm, what proportion of test sessions reach H = 1?
RQ3: Does a blind CoE panel certify sessions reliably?
RQ4: Does the physiology-only S2C index show convergent validity
     with self-reported stress?
RQ5: Does HOW raise mean in-window elevation E relative to the
     active control?

------------------------------------------------------------
2. HYPOTHESES
------------------------------------------------------------
H1 (PRIMARY, confirmatory): The probability of H = 1 per test
    session is higher in HOW than in Active Control (AC).
    Smallest effect size of interest (SESOI): d = 0.50 on the
    person-level H-rate (proportion of the 5 sessions with H = 1).
H2 (benchmark): In the HOW arm, the session-level H = 1 rate is
    ≥ .70 (point estimate), with 95% CI lower bound > .30.
H3 (instrument): CoE certification reliability ICC(2,k) ≥ .80.
H4 (instrument): S2C correlates with momentary stress VAS at
    r_rm ≥ .30 (repeated-measures correlation), and person-level
    mean S2C correlates with baseline PSS-10 at r ≥ .30.
    (Physiology–self-report concordance is typically modest;
    thresholds are set accordingly.)
H5 (secondary): Mean in-window E is higher in HOW than AC,
    d ≥ 0.50.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. STUDY DESIGN
------------------------------------------------------------
Design: 3-arm parallel randomized controlled trial.
Arms:
 (1) HOW — 7-day Purist Form (PF) training (see §5.3).
 (2) Active Control (AC) — 7-day attention-matched program:
     guided progressive muscle relaxation + general well-being
     psychoeducation; identical contact time, materials format,
     facilitator contact, and session schedule; contains no PF
     criteria.
 (3) Waitlist (WL) — no training during the trial; completes all
     assessments; offered HOW training after follow-up.
Phases (all arms):
 - Days 1–14: Baseline EMA (4×/day) + baseline questionnaires.
 - Days 15–21: Training (HOW or AC) / waiting (WL).
 - Days 22–26: 5 × 30-min lab test sessions (one per day),
   identical protocol in all arms.
 - Day 40: Follow-up questionnaires + 1 follow-up test session
   (exploratory durability).
Randomization: Computer-generated, permuted blocks (size 3 or 6,
 random), 1:1:1, stratified by baseline M (median split).
 Allocation concealed by a third party until after baseline.
Blinding: Participants not blind to arm content. Expectancy
 measured (CEQ) and used as a covariate in sensitivity analysis.
 CoE raters, outcome assessors, and analysts blind to arm.
 Analysis code finalized on a masked dataset (arms recoded A/B/C)
 before unblinding.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. SAMPLING PLAN
------------------------------------------------------------
Power: Two-sided α = .05, power = .80, d = 0.50 (HOW vs AC on
 person-level H-rate) requires n = 64 per arm. (Note: v1.0's
 n = 30/arm gives power ≈ .48.) Allowing 15% attrition:
 n = 76 per arm; N = 228 randomized. The person-level test
 ignores within-person repeated sessions, so this estimate is
 conservative for the mixed-effects primary model.
Inclusion: Age 18–65; fluent in study language; able to attend
 6 lab sessions.
Exclusion: Current psychiatric disorder under active treatment;
 PHQ-9 ≥ 15 or GAD-7 ≥ 15 at screening (referred to care);
 current substance use disorder; psychoactive medication
 affecting HRV (e.g., beta-blockers); cardiac arrhythmia;
 pregnancy; meditation or contemplative practice > 1 hr/day.
Recruitment: [source]. Compensation: [amount, schedule — not
 contingent on outcomes].
Interim analysis: One, at N = 114 completers (50%), by an
 independent monitor, safety and instrument checks only (no
 efficacy look). Stop if (a) adverse-event rate > 10% in any arm,
 or (b) CoE ICC(2,k) < .60 after one rater-retraining cycle.
Adverse event (AE): Any of: panic or dissociative episode;
 self-reported distress ≥ baseline M_stress + 2 SD_b; participant-
 reported harm; withdrawal for distress. Screened after every
 session; serious AEs reported to IRB within 48 h.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. VARIABLES AND PROTOCOL
------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Momentary instruments (baseline EMA AND in-window probes;
    same items so baseline and test are on one scale)
- PA: I-PANAS-SF positive-affect items, momentary instruction
  (Thompson, 2007).
- FLOW: Short Flow State Scale-2, 9 items (Jackson, Martin &
  Eklund, 2008).
- G_m (momentary goodness): 3 items, 1–7, authored for this
  study, adapted from the elevation literature (Haidt, 2003;
  Algoe & Haidt, 2009): "Right now I feel uplifted toward the
  good"; "Right now I am acting from my own values";
  "Right now my sense of goodness needs nothing added."
  Reliability (ω) and factor structure reported.
- STRESS: Stress VAS, 0–100.

5.2 Baseline / follow-up questionnaires (trait-level; NOT used
    inside the 30-minute window)
- PSS-10 (Cohen & Williamson, 1988)
- WHO-5 (WHO, 1998; Topp et al., 2015)
- Strengths Use Scale (Govindji & Linley, 2007)
- PHQ-9, GAD-7 (screening)
- Credibility/Expectancy Questionnaire, CEQ (Devilly &
  Borkovec, 2000) — after training day 1 and day 7.

5.3 Purist Form (PF) — five preregistered criteria
Derived clause-by-clause from Def-Rel v1.0; author confirms
before filing:
 PF-1 Floor named: participant states their own baseline
      (mediocrity) state at session start.
 PF-2 Present-bound: attention held on the current moment; no
      planning toward external reward.
 PF-3 Non-dependence: no seeking of external input, including
      appeal to forces greater than self, to produce the state.
 PF-4 Self-sourced Goodness: at least one internally generated
      value-act or value-intention recorded.
 PF-5 Overlay, not replacement: reality appraisal intact (post-
      session reality-check items answered accurately).
PF fidelity = criteria met / 5, scored by a blind rater from the
 structured post-session report and probe responses. Fidelity
 ≥ .80 means ≥ 4 of 5. Scored identically in all arms.

5.4 Zero-external-input protocol (X)
Session: single-occupant, sound-attenuated room; fixed lighting
 and temperature; seated.
Prohibited in window: phone, watch, books, music, food, drink
 (water allowed), conversation, experimenter contact. No
 caffeine or nicotine 2 h before the session.
Permitted measurement contact: wearable sensors; tablet probes at
 t = 10, 20, 30 min with fixed, content-neutral wording (declared
 measurement, not input).
Compliance: door/occupancy log, tablet interaction log, and a
 post-session compliance checklist. Any breach → X = 1.
Training (days 15–21) is preparation, not in-window input.

5.5 Physiology
- HRV-RMSSD: Polar H10 chest strap (RR intervals), 1-min epochs.
- EDA (tonic SCL): Shimmer3 GSR+ or equivalent research-grade
  device (Empatica E4 is discontinued).
Physiology is standardized against a 5-min seated rest at the
 start of each session.

5.6 Operational definitions
Baseline (per person, from 14-day EMA, ≈ 56 observations):
 Composite C = mean[ z(PA), z(FLOW), z(G_m), −z(STRESS) ],
  with z computed against the person's own baseline mean and SD.
 M    = median of baseline C (the "mediocrity" floor).
 SD_b = within-person SD of baseline C.
In-window elevation:
 E(t) = C computed at probe t ∈ {10, 20, 30}, using the same
  baseline standardization.
Physiological stress index (no self-report items):
 S(t) = mean[ −z(RMSSD), z(SCL) ], 1-min resolution.
 S2C  = deterministic CIELAB map of S (preregistered lookup
  table and code), rendered as a 30-min color timeline.
Primary outcome (per session):
 H = 1 iff ALL of:
  (a) E(t) ≥ M + 1·SD_b at all three probes t = 10, 20, 30;
  (b) X = 0;
  (c) PF fidelity ≥ .80;
  (d) CoE certification = 1.
 Otherwise H = 0. Computed identically in all arms.
Secondary:
 Ē = mean of E(10), E(20), E(30) (continuous).
 G_m (mean of in-window probes).
 S2C session mean.
 PF fidelity (continuous).
Exploratory: raw RMSSD, SCL; follow-up session H and Ē; WHO-5,
 PSS-10, Strengths Use change baseline → follow-up.

5.7 Center-of-Excellence (CoE) certification
Panel: 3 raters, trained on a separate pilot set, no role in
 training delivery, no conflicts of interest.
Input per session (arm-masked, order randomized): S2C color
 timeline, probe values with threshold line, compliance log.
Rating: "Elevation held across the window," 1–7.
Certification: CoE = 1 iff panel mean ≥ 5.
Reliability: ICC(2,k) on the 1–7 ratings.

------------------------------------------------------------
6. ANALYSIS PLAN
------------------------------------------------------------
Primary (H1): Mixed-effects logistic regression,
 H ~ arm + baseline-M stratum + session_index + (1 | subject),
 contrast HOW vs AC; reported as OR with 95% CI. Single primary
 test, α = .05 two-sided; no multiplicity correction.
 Person-level H-rate comparison (Cohen's d) reported alongside.
H2: Session-level H rate in HOW with cluster-robust 95% CI.
H3: ICC(2,k), two-way random, absolute agreement, with 95% CI
 (R package irr or psych).
H4: Repeated-measures correlation S2C vs STRESS VAS at probe
 times (rmcorr; Bakdash & Marusich, 2017); Pearson r between
 person-mean S2C and baseline PSS-10.
H5: Linear mixed model, Ē ~ arm + stratum + session_index +
 (1 | subject); HOW vs AC, Cohen's d from model-estimated means.
Secondary family (H2, H4, H5, other secondary contrasts incl.
 WL): Benjamini–Hochberg FDR at q = .05.
Missing data: Intention-to-treat on all randomized. Multiple
 imputation by chained equations, m = 20, including arm, stratum,
 baseline M, CEQ. Sessions breaching X are H = 0 in ITT.
Sensitivity: (a) per-protocol (X = 0 sessions, ≥ 4 training
 days); (b) CEQ expectancy as covariate; (c) H without the CoE
 criterion (d); (d) H with ≥ 2 of 3 probes.
Software: R 4.x (lme4, irr, psych, rmcorr, mice); code and
 masked-data dry run posted before unblinding.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. DECISION RULES (preregistered outcome classes)
------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1 — Instrument validity check:
 INCONCLUSIVE (instrument failure) if CoE ICC(2,k) < .60, OR
 the 95% CI upper bound of S2C–STRESS r_rm < .20.
 → No verdict on HOW; the measurement failed, not the theory.
Step 2 — If instruments are valid:
 SUPPORTED if H1 is significant (HOW > AC) AND H2 is met
  (H rate ≥ .70, lower CI > .30).
 FALSIFIED if HOW session H rate < .30, OR the HOW vs AC effect
  is d < .20 with 90% CI upper bound < .50 (equivalence-type
  test against the SESOI).
 INDETERMINATE otherwise (e.g., H rate between .30 and .70, or
  an effect too imprecise to fall in either class). Reported as
  such, with estimates; no claim either way.

8. OTHER
------------------------------------------------------------
Limitations:
 - X = 0 is enforced and logged, not metaphysically proven;
   probes are a declared minimal measurement contact.
 - PF is author-defined; PF criteria are fixed here before data.
 - CoE independence is structural (blinding, no COI), not
   institutional accreditation.
 - S2C is a physiological proxy for stress, not a direct measure
   of "Goodness."
 - H is binary by decision; continuous Ē is reported throughout.
 - Participants cannot be blind to arm; expectancy is measured.
 - G_m items are new; psychometrics reported, not assumed.
Deviations: Time-stamped deviations log, reported in full.
Conflicts of interest: The first author originated the HOW
 framework (ERES corpus). Mitigations: blind raters and analysts,
 masked-data analysis, independent safety monitor, open data
 and code. [Declare any further interests.]
